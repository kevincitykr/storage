# 🎨 Designer — 영상 기획 및 썸네일 디자인

## Design Master Plan

**Goals:**

* Establish consistent design guidelines and templates.
* Automate design processes for efficiency.
* Improve brand identity through visuals.

**Action Steps:**

**1. Review Existing Design Materials:**
* Assess existing brand assets and guidelines.
* Analyze past design projects and identify areas for improvement.
* Gather feedback from stakeholders on desired design direction.

**2. Develop Design System:**
* Create a comprehensive design system containing reusable elements, such as logos, typography, color palette, and imagery.
* Establish clear guidelines for using design elements consistently.
* Develop templates for common layouts and content structures.

**3. Automate Design Processes:**
* Utilize design automation tools to streamline tasks such as logo creation, typography selection, and color palette generation.
* Automate design review and approval processes.

**4. Implement Design Principles:**
* Establish clear design principles to guide decision-making.
* Ensure consistency in design across all platforms and projects.
* Seek inspiration from industry best practices and trends.

**5. Continuous Improvement:**
* Regularly review and update design guidelines and templates.
* Track and analyze design performance to identify areas for improvement.

**Resources:**

* Design software (Figma, Adobe Creative Suite)
* Design automation tools (Autodesk Substance Painter, Adobe XD)
* Design inspiration websites (Pinterest, Behance, Dribbble)

**Timeline:**

* Phase 1: Review and Analysis - 1 week
* Phase 2: Design System Development - 2 weeks
* Phase 3: Automation Implementation - 2 weeks
* Phase 4: Implementation and Review - 2 weeks
* Phase 5: Continuous Improvement - Ongoing

**Success Metrics:**

* Improved brand consistency and recognition.
* Increased efficiency and productivity.
* Enhanced visual storytelling and user engagement.
