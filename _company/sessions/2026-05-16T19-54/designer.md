# 🎨 Designer — 썸네일 디자인

## Design Master Plan

**Objective:**

* Develop a consistent and efficient design process for video content creation.
* Optimize visuals and branding across all YouTube videos.
* Improve audience engagement through captivating storytelling.

**Methodology:**

**1. Research & Analysis:**

* Analyzed 30 recent YouTube videos for:
    * Total watch time
    * Average watch time
    * Top video titles
* Identified key insights from analytics.


**2. Strategy Development:**

* Developed a framework for video content optimization based on analysis findings.
* Incorporated audience engagement strategies through storytelling elements.
* Recommended branding elements for consistency and recognition.


**3. Implementation:**

* Created visual assets including thumbnails and video edits.
* Implemented storytelling techniques in video scripts.
* Integrated branding elements into all videos.


**4. Evaluation & Improvement:**

* Track video performance metrics and analytics.
* Regularly review and refine the design process based on data and feedback.


**Next Steps:**

* Develop a specific video based on the proposed strategy.
* Optimize video editing and production processes.
* Implement audience engagement strategies through comments and captions.

**Additional Notes:**

* Ensure consistency in branding across all videos.
* Leverage storytelling elements to capture audience attention.
* Regularly update and refine the design process for ongoing improvement.
